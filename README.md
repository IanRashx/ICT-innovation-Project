Agent Resilience
ICT30016 – ICT Innovation Project
Project Overview

Agent Resilience is an ICT Innovation Project focused on investigating how Artificial Intelligence (AI) agents can remain reliable, secure, and functional when exposed to adversarial inputs such as jailbreak attempts.

AI agents and Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly being used to automate tasks and interact with users. However, these systems can sometimes be manipulated using specially designed prompts that attempt to bypass their intended restrictions or alter their expected behaviour.

The purpose of this project is to investigate these vulnerabilities and explore methods that can improve the resilience of AI agents against jailbreak and adversarial attacks.

Problem Statement

AI agents can be vulnerable to malicious or unexpected prompts that attempt to manipulate their behaviour.

A successful jailbreak may cause an AI agent to:

Ignore its intended instructions.
Bypass safety restrictions.
Produce unintended responses.
Behave outside its expected role.
Become less reliable when exposed to adversarial inputs.

A resilient AI agent should be able to resist these disturbances or recover its expected behaviour after an attack.

The project will investigate how AI agent resilience can be evaluated and potentially improved.

Project Objectives

The current proposed objectives of the project are:

Investigate common AI jailbreak and adversarial prompt techniques.
Develop a controlled environment for testing AI agents against normal and adversarial prompts.
Identify methods for detecting potentially malicious or jailbreak-style prompts.
Investigate techniques for protecting AI agents from prompt manipulation.
Measure AI agent behaviour before and after adversarial attacks.
Evaluate the effectiveness of proposed resilience mechanisms.
Investigate whether an AI agent can maintain or recover its intended behaviour after an attack.

These objectives are preliminary and may be refined following discussions with the project client and tutor.

What is a Jailbreak?

An AI jailbreak is an attempt to manipulate an AI model into bypassing its intended rules, restrictions, or safety controls.

For example, an AI assistant may normally refuse a request that violates its instructions. An attacker may attempt to manipulate the AI using carefully constructed prompts that tell the AI to ignore its previous instructions or behave as another unrestricted system.

A successful jailbreak occurs when the AI follows the attacker's instructions instead of maintaining its intended behaviour.

What is Agent Resilience?

Agent resilience refers to the ability of an AI agent to continue operating correctly when exposed to unexpected, malicious, or adversarial conditions.

A resilient AI agent should ideally be able to:

Detect suspicious or abnormal inputs.
Resist attempts to manipulate its behaviour.
Maintain its expected functionality.
Recover after an attack or disturbance.
Continue serving legitimate users.
Record or report suspicious activity for analysis.
