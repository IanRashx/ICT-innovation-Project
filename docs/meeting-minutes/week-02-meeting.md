# Topics Discussed

## 1. Agent Resilience

The team discussed Agent Resilience as the ability of an AI agent to remain
reliable when exposed to malicious or unexpected inputs.

The team discussed the importance of resistance and recovery when an AI agent is
exposed to adversarial conditions.

---

## 2. Jailbreak Attacks

The team discussed AI jailbreak attacks as attempts to manipulate an AI system
into bypassing its intended instructions, restrictions or expected behaviour.

The team agreed that jailbreak attacks are an important area to investigate as
part of the project.

---

## 3. Project Problem

The preliminary problem identified by the team is that AI agents can potentially
be manipulated through adversarial prompts.

If an AI agent cannot resist or recover from these interactions, its reliability
and security may be reduced.

---

## 4. Preliminary Project Direction

The team discussed a possible project direction involving:

- Normal prompt testing.
- Jailbreak prompt testing.
- Jailbreak detection.
- Resilience mechanisms.
- Output analysis.
- Logging.
- Evaluation.
- Comparison of agent behaviour before and after protection.

This direction must be confirmed with the tutor/client.
