## Project Background

Artificial Intelligence agents and Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly
used to automate tasks, interact with users and support decision-making.

However, AI agents can be exposed to adversarial inputs such as jailbreak
attempts. These attacks attempt to manipulate an AI model into ignoring its
intended instructions, restrictions or expected behaviour.

If an AI agent cannot resist or recover from these disturbances, its reliability
and security may be reduced.

The Agent Resilience project focuses on investigating how AI agents behave when
exposed to adversarial conditions and how their resilience can be evaluated and
potentially improved.

---

## What is Agent Resilience?

Agent Resilience refers to the ability of an AI agent to continue operating
reliably when exposed to unexpected, malicious or adversarial conditions.

A resilient AI agent should ideally be able to:

- Maintain its intended behaviour.
- Resist malicious manipulation.
- Detect abnormal or suspicious interactions.
- Reduce the impact of attacks.
- Recover after a disturbance.
- Continue operating for legitimate users.

---

## What is an AI Jailbreak?

An AI jailbreak is an attempt to manipulate an AI model so that it bypasses its
intended rules, restrictions or safety controls.

For example, an attacker may create a prompt that attempts to make an AI agent
ignore its original instructions and follow new malicious instructions.

A successful jailbreak occurs when the AI agent behaves outside its intended
rules or restrictions.

---

## Problem Statement

AI agents may be vulnerable to jailbreak and adversarial prompts that attempt to
change their intended behaviour.

This creates a problem for organisations and developers who need AI systems to
remain reliable and secure even when malicious users attempt to manipulate them.

There is therefore a need to investigate how AI agents react to adversarial
inputs and how resilience can be measured and improved.

---

## Project Objectives

The preliminary objectives of this project are:

1. Understand AI Agent Resilience and its importance.
2. Investigate common jailbreak and adversarial prompt techniques.
3. Examine how jailbreak attacks affect AI agent behaviour.
4. Identify possible methods for detecting suspicious prompts.
5. Investigate possible defence and resilience mechanisms.
6. Develop a controlled environment for evaluating AI agent behaviour.
7. Compare AI agent performance before and after resilience mechanisms are applied.
8. Develop measurable methods for evaluating agent resilience.
