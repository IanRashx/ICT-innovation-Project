# Agent Resilience – Stakeholder Analysis

## Purpose

This document identifies the preliminary users, stakeholders and stakeholder
needs for the Agent Resilience project.

The stakeholder analysis will be updated after further consultation with the
project tutor/client.

---

## Primary Users

The preliminary primary users are:

### AI Developers

AI developers may need to determine whether their AI agents continue to behave
correctly when exposed to malicious or unexpected prompts.

### AI Security Researchers

Researchers may need to investigate vulnerabilities in AI agents and evaluate
different resilience approaches.

### Cybersecurity Researchers

Cybersecurity researchers may use the system to investigate jailbreak,
adversarial and prompt-based attacks against AI systems.

---

## Other Stakeholders

Other potential stakeholders include:

- Project client.
- ICT30016 teaching staff.
- Organisations deploying AI agents.
- AI system administrators.
- Software developers.
- Security teams.
- Researchers.
- End users interacting with AI systems.

---

# Stakeholder Needs

## Need 1 – Agent Reliability

### Need

A way to determine whether an AI agent continues to behave as intended when
exposed to jailbreak or adversarial prompts.

### Stakeholders

- AI developers
- Researchers
- Organisations deploying AI

### Reason

AI agents may behave differently when exposed to malicious or unexpected inputs.

---

## Need 2 – Identification of Suspicious Interactions

### Need

A way to identify when an interaction may be attempting to manipulate an AI
agent outside its intended behaviour.

### Stakeholders

- AI developers
- Cybersecurity researchers
- System administrators

### Reason

Jailbreak attempts may be designed specifically to change or bypass the intended
behaviour of an AI system.

---

## Need 3 – Reduction of Attack Impact

### Need

A way to reduce the impact of malicious or adversarial prompts on the AI agent.

### Stakeholders

- Organisations deploying AI agents
- AI developers
- Security teams

### Reason

An AI agent should remain reliable even when exposed to abnormal or malicious
inputs.

---

## Need 4 – Resilience Measurement

### Need

A way to measure and compare AI agent behaviour before, during and after
adversarial interaction.

### Stakeholders

- Researchers
- Developers
- Project client

### Reason

Measurable results are required to determine whether a resilience mechanism
actually improves AI agent behaviour.

---

## Need 5 – Continued Legitimate Use

### Need

A way to provide security protection without unnecessarily preventing legitimate
users from interacting with the AI system.

### Stakeholders

- End users
- Organisations
- Developers

### Reason

A security mechanism that blocks legitimate interactions may reduce the
usefulness of the system.

---

# Preliminary Main Process

The main process supported by the proposed project is:

1. A test prompt is provided to an AI agent.
2. The input is analysed.
3. Potentially suspicious behaviour is identified.
4. A resilience or protection mechanism may be applied.
5. The AI agent generates a response.
6. The response is evaluated.
7. Test results are recorded.
8. The resilience of the AI agent is measured.
